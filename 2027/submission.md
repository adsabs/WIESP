## Paper types

**Long papers:** up to 8 pages of content (9 in the camera-ready version), plus unlimited pages of references.

**Short papers:** up to 4 pages of content (5 in the camera-ready version), plus unlimited pages of references.

The extra page in the camera-ready version is for addressing the reviewers' comments.

## Formatting

Submissions must follow the host venue's formatting guidelines and use the official [ACL template files](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files).

## Review process

All submissions undergo **double-blind** peer review. Submissions must be anonymised: do not include author names, affiliations or acknowledgements, and avoid self-references that reveal your identity.

## Policies

WASP 2027 follows the [NAACL 2027 policies](https://2027.naacl.org/calls/main_conference_papers/) on anonymity, preprints, and double submissions.

## Submission site

Papers are submitted through **OpenReview**. The submission link will be posted here when submissions open.

## Shared task papers

System description papers for the WASP 2027 shared task undergo light peer review and appear in the proceedings. Details will be given in the shared task's call for papers.

## Publication and presentation

Accepted papers will appear in the WASP 2027 proceedings in the [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org). The workshop is held **in person** at NAACL 2027 in San Francisco.

See [Important Dates](important_dates) for all deadlines.
