## Registration

WASP 2026 is co-located with IJCNLP-AACL 2026 and is held **fully online**. The main conference handles registration, not the workshop. At least one author of each accepted paper is expected to register so the paper can be presented and included in the proceedings.

Registration details, fees and the registration portal are on the [IJCNLP-AACL 2026 website](https://2026.aaclnet.org/).
