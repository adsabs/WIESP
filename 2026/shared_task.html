    Evaluation of Multimodal Capabilities on Scientific Figures

    Motivation

    Scientists rely on figures to share their discoveries, but this makes the information contained in the figures hard to parse, archive, and search. Recent multimodal models promise to extract this information. We propose a dataset and an associated task to evaluate a model’s multimodal capabilities by its ability to partially reconstruct the citation graph of scientific papers from their figures.

    Task Description

    The goal of the task is simple classification.
    The inputs are pairs of figures, a figure and a caption, or a pair of captions.
    The possible classes are:

    • “same paper”;
    • “different paper but related (cited/citing)”;
    • “unrelated”;
    • “same figure” (only for figure+caption pairs).
