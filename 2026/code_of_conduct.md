WASP 2026 is an ACL workshop held as part of IJCNLP-AACL 2026. All participants (authors, presenters, reviewers, organizers and attendees) are expected to follow the ACL's policies, including at online sessions and in workshop-related discussions:

- [ACL Anti-Harassment Policy](https://www.aclweb.org/adminwiki/index.php?title=Anti-Harassment_Policy)
- [IJCNLP-AACL 2026 Code of Conduct](https://2026.aaclnet.org/participants/anti_harassment/), which includes the ACL Code of Conduct and the conference's anti-harassment policy

The ACL is dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone. Harassment and hostile behaviour are not welcome at any ACL event, including online events like this workshop.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness harassment or hostile behaviour, you can contact any member of the ACL **Professional Conduct Committee**, as described on the [IJCNLP-AACL 2026 Code of Conduct page](https://2026.aaclnet.org/participants/anti_harassment/). Members of the committee keep such contacts in strict confidence.

You can also contact the workshop organizers at [atilla.alkan@cfa.harvard.edu](mailto:atilla.alkan@cfa.harvard.edu).
