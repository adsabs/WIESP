    Tuesday, November 10, 2026 (local time in China, UTC+8) · Online · Zoom link: TBA

    All times are China Standard Time (UTC+8), the official time zone of the workshop. Each session also lists its start and end times in US Eastern (ET), Central European (CET) and US Pacific (PT) time. On 10 November, CST = ET + 13h = CET + 7h = PT + 16h, so the ET times of Sessions 1–2 and the PT times of Sessions 1–3 fall on 9 November.

    The program may be subject to small changes.
